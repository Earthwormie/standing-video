# ISEE 2027 参会邀请函模板

面向 ISEE 各区域分会会长的群发邀请函（英文）。每封仅需替换 `{{LastName}}`；联系方式每封相同。

---

## 正文模板（直接复制）

**Subject:** Invitation to the 19th ISEE Biennial Conference — Beijing, May 2027

Dear Professor {{LastName}},

I hope this message finds you well. It is my great pleasure to invite you to the **19th Conference of the International Society for Ecological Economics**, to be held in **Beijing from 5–9 May 2027**, hosted by the **School of Economics, Peking University**, together with the Chinese Society of Ecological Economics and ISEE.

The conference convenes under the theme **"Harmony with Nature, Harmony among Territories: Ecological Civilization for a Peaceful, Just and Sustainable Future."** Building on the eighteenth Conference in Oslo, this edition turns to the theory and economics of the ecological-civilization paradigm, the mainstreaming of ecological economics in socioeconomic governance, and sustainable development across the Global South — alongside global commons and biodiversity, energy transition under a changing climate, environmental justice and political ecology, the ecological footprint of AI and digital transformation, agroecology and food-system transition, and human well-being within planetary boundaries. The program will span keynote plenaries, parallel sessions, roundtables, and "Meet the Masters" dialogues, and will close with the conferral of the Society's principal awards — the **Kenneth E. Boulding Memorial Award** and the **Bina Agarwal Prize for Young Scholars**.

Your presence would mean a great deal — both to the participants and to the standing of the meeting — and we would be truly honored to welcome you to Beijing. Once you are able to confirm your participation, we will follow up with the detailed day-by-day program, along with travel and accommodation arrangements; please let me know if there is anything that would help you take part.

On a related note, I also lead the **Center for Human and Economic Development Studies (CHEDS) at Peking University**, which is forming an international Academic Advisory Committee. Given your standing in the field, we would be delighted to invite you to serve as an honorary academic advisor — a nominal role with no obligations — and, should you be willing, to present the appointment during your visit for the conference. I would be happy to say more if you are interested.

It would be a genuine pleasure to host you in Beijing in 2027.

With warm regards,

Xi Ji
Professor, School of Economics, Peking University
Director, Center for Human and Economic Development Studies (CHEDS)
Host, 19th ISEE Biennial Conference

## 核对记录

- **会议**：国际生态经济学会第十九届大会 / 19th Conference of the International Society for Ecological Economics
- **时间地点**：2027年5月5日–9日，北京
- **主办**：北京大学经济学院；合办：中国生态经济学学会、ISEE
- **奖项名称已核实**：Kenneth E. Boulding Memorial Award（终身成就）；Bina Agarwal Prize for Young Scholars（青年学者，以 Bina Agarwal 命名，非 Anil Agarwal）。故正文用中性的 "the Society's principal awards"，未称"最高荣誉"。
- **中心英文名**：Center for Human and Economic Development Studies (CHEDS), Peking University（官方缩写，域名 cheds.pku.edu.cn）
- **收件人身份**：ISEE 无 "Regional Chair" 头衔；对应的是各区域分会会长（President of Regional Societies）。按用户要求，信中不点明对方身份，仅作邀请。
